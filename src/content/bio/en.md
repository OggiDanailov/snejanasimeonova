title: Biography
---
Snejana Hristova Simeonova was born in Sofia, Bulgaria. She graduated from the National Academy of Fine Arts in Sofia with an MA in Sculpture.

## Collections

Her works are held by the National Gallery in Sofia; the Sofia City Art Gallery; the art galleries in Plovdiv, Varna, Dobrich and elsewhere; the Bulgarian Ministry of Culture; the Pushkin Art Gallery, Moscow; the Union of Polish Sculptors; the Rock Museum in Iwase, Japan; the Municipality of Maalot, Israel; the Hugo Voeten Collection, Belgium; the Municipality of Obernkirchen, Germany; and private collections in Bulgaria, the USA, Germany, Switzerland, the Czech Republic, Argentina, Greece, Australia, the Netherlands, South Africa, New Zealand and elsewhere.
